# Regulatory Workflows and Interactions (RWI) - pharma
Regulatory exchange is globally fragmented across authorities, product lines, procedures, and technologies. This IG establishes a common Task- and Subscription-based framework for regulatory workflows and interactions, enabling them to be exchanged, tracked, and acted upon with machine-readable status and event-driven notifications.

Scope (current phase): regulatory workflows and interactions between applicants and regulatory authorities for human biopharmaceutical products. Medical devices, veterinary drugs, OTC drugs, natural health products, food, cosmetics, and inspections are out of scope for this phase.

The project is currently slated for the January 2027 Ballot cycle, and the ballot level is STU.

Link to the IG: https://build.fhir.org/ig/HL7/APIX---API-Exchange-for-Medicinal-Products/branches/main/index.html
