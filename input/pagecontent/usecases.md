APIX replaces today’s fragmented, procedure-specific exchange mechanisms with **one single, unified API doorway** for everything.

### Current Reality – A Maze of Separate Doorways
Today, regulatory procedures require different formats (e.g., eCTD, non-eCTD) and live behind multiple isolated entrances (e.g., FTP, portals, AS2 gateways, EVWEB, or E2B-specific portals, secure email, email, physical mail).  

The result for regulators and companies alike, is dozens of different formats, logins, validation rules, status-tracking tools, and acknowledgement workflows. Every new technical doorway forces another custom integration, training program, delay, and added cost.

### With APIX – One Doorway for Everything
**One FHIR API. One authentication flow. One set of patterns.**  
The same doorway handles **all** of the use cases below — and any future procedure added later.

### Supported Use Cases (All via the Same Doorway)

1. **Post-Authorization Procedures** – All variation types, grouping, worksharing, renewals  
2. **Pharmacovigilance & ICSR** – E2B(R3)-compatible safety reports and follow-ups  
3. **Electronic Product Information (ePI) & Labeling** – Structured submission, negotiation, and dissemination  
4. **Clinical Trial Applications** – IND/CTA/IMPD, amendments, DSUR, safety reporting  
5. **Health Technology Assessment (HTA)** – Structured evidence dossiers for reimbursement  
6. **Inspections & Establishment Registration** – GMP/GCP/GLP facilities, inspection workflows, CAPA  
7. **Master Files & Certificates** – DMF, PMF, VAMF, CEP submissions and updates  
8. **Cross-Border Procedures** – MRP/DCP, worksharing, simultaneous/collaborative multi-agency review  
9.  **Marketing Authorization Applications** – Initial MAA/NDA/NDS and full assessment lifecycle
10. **Emergency & Accelerated Pathways** – Rolling review, conditional authorization, compassionate use
