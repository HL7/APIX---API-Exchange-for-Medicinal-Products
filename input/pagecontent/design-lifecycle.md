## Lifecycle Design

This section provides design guidance for managing the lifecycle of regulatory submissions in the APIX ecosystem.

### Overview

The lifecycle design addresses the complete journey of a medicinal product from initial submission through post-authorization monitoring.

### Lifecycle Phases

1. **Pre-Submission**: Preparation and submission planning
2. **Validation**: Technical and administrative validation
3. **Assessment**: Scientific and regulatory review
4. **Decision**: Final regulatory determination
5. **Post-Approval**: Ongoing compliance and reporting

### Design Considerations

- **State Management**: Clear definition of workflow states and transitions
- **Clock Management**: Regulatory timeline management (clock stops, restarts)
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive tracking of all submissions and responses
- **Audit Trail**: Complete history of all regulatory actions
