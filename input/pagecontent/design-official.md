## Official Design

This section provides design guidance for official regulatory communications in the APIX ecosystem.

### Overview

Official design covers the formal presentation of regulatory decisions, approvals, and communications that carry legal and regulatory significance.

### Design Requirements

1. **Authenticity**: Digital signatures and authentication mechanisms
2. **Formality**: Professional, standardized presentation formats
3. **Legality**: Compliance with regulatory legal requirements
4. **Permanence**: Long-term preservation and accessibility

### Key Elements

- **Decision Letter**: Official regulatory decision document
- **Approval Certificate**: Legal authorization document
- **Regulatory References**: Citations to applicable regulations
- **Effective Dates**: Legal effectiveness dates for decisions

### Implementation Guidance

- Use standardized templates for consistency
- Implement digital signature verification
- Maintain complete audit trails
- Ensure long-term archival capabilities
