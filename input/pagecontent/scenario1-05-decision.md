## Scenario 1: Decision (Final Regulatory Decision)

This scenario demonstrates the final regulatory decision in the APIX workflow, representing the conclusion of the review process.

### Task Overview

| Element | Value |
|---------|-------|
| **Task ID** | scenario1-05-decision |
| **Task Status** | completed |
| **Business Status** | Approved |
| **Task Code** | approval |

### Context

After completion of the regulatory assessment and review of all submitted information, the regulatory authority issues the final decision. In this scenario, a positive decision (approval) is issued to the applicant.

### Key Information

- **Decision Type**: Approval of the marketing authorization application or variation
- **Effective Date**: The approval letter specifies the effective date of authorization
- **Conditions**: Any post-approval commitments or conditions may be specified
- **Publishing**: The decision is typically published in the regulatory authority's public database

### Possible Outcomes

- **Approved**: Product granted marketing authorization
- **Rejected**: Application not approved; applicant may appeal or resubmit
- **Conditional Approval**: Approval contingent on fulfillment of specific commitments
- **Deferred**: Decision deferred pending additional information or assessment

### Next Steps

Upon approval, the product enters the post-authorization phase with ongoing pharmacovigilance, periodic reporting, and potential variations.
